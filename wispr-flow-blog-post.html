I Wrote a Whole Blog Post Without Touching a Keyboard.

3 March 2026 • Productivity

Most people think writing means sitting at a desk, staring at a screen, typing words. That's one way to do it. It's also the slowest, most painful way possible.

I've been writing my blog posts by talking them out loud with Wispr Flow. Every single word. Zero typing.

Here's how it works:

  • Open Wispr Flow
  • Start talking through my main points
  • Edit lightly afterwards (maybe 5 minutes of cleanup)
  • Done

The results:

  • 800 words in 25 minutes (including editing)
  • Sounds like me - conversational, casual, actual voice
  • Way less fatigue - talking is easier than typing

The magic isn't just speed. It's that my writing sounds like a person wrote it. Shocking, I know. When I type, everything comes out formal and stiff. When I talk, I actually sound like a human being.

I've written 5 blog posts this week this way. That's more than I wrote last month.

If you're trying to build an audience or share ideas, your writing voice matters. Typing kills it. Talking preserves it.

Start talking. Start writing.
