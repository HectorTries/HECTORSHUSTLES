I Dictate My LinkedIn Posts While Walking. They Perform Better Than the Ones I Type.

12 September 2026 • Productivity

I used to stare at the LinkedIn box for 20 minutes. Delete. Rewrite. Sound too corporate. Sound too casual. Post nothing.

Now I post 4x a week, in about 10 minutes total. All dictated. Most of them while I'm out walking.

Here's the exact workflow.

The problem with typing LinkedIn posts

When I type, I self-edit. I write like I'm applying for a job. No stories, no edge, no me.

When I talk, I actually sound like me. And on LinkedIn right now, human beats polished.

Voice was always the answer. The friction was transcription — Apple dictation garbling words, re-typing everything anyway. Not worth it.

Then I switched to Wispr Flow.

My 10-minute walk-and-post system

  • Walk and rant. I hold a thought — "what I learned fixing a client bug" — and just talk it out for 2 minutes. Wispr Flow transcribes straight into LinkedIn / my notes app, with proper punctuation and no "uhs."
  • Say the formatting. I literally say "new line, hook first" and clean it up by voice. Wispr Flow is built for this — it understands commands, fixes grammar, keeps my tone.
  • Post same-day. No drafts folder where posts go to die. If it took longer than 3 minutes to say, it's too long. Cut and ship.

Last week: 4 posts, ~18,000 impressions, 2 inbound DMs that turned into calls. Total writing time: under 40 minutes for the week.

Compare that to my old system: 1 post every two weeks, 400 impressions, endless guilt.

Why Wispr Flow, not phone dictation

Three things sold me:

  • Accuracy with accents and fast speech. I'm Welsh, I talk fast on walks with wind. It still gets it right.
  • It works everywhere. LinkedIn, Gmail, Notion, X — press a hotkey and dictate. No copy-paste dance.
  • Edits by voice. Say "make it shorter" or "more casual" and it rewrites. That's the killer feature for posts.

I'm not a "thought leader." I'm just documenting work out loud. That's what performs now.

If you want to post consistently without becoming a content person, try dictating instead of typing.
