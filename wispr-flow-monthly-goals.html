I Planned My Whole Month in 10 Minutes — By Talking Out Loud

1 October 2026 • Productivity

Every month starts the same way. I open a blank page, write "October Goals" at the top, and then… stare at it. What did I actually want to do this month? What carried over from September? What matters and what's just noise?

Last month that blank page sat open for three days. I finally filled it in during a boring Zoom call. Not exactly intentional goal-setting.

This month I tried something different. I opened Wispr Flow, hit the dictation hotkey, and just talked through my month out loud.

The 10-minute brain dump

Here's what I actually said, roughly: "Okay, October. Big rock is launching the new hustle page… need to finish the wedding venue shortlist… gym three times a week, no excuses… want to read two books… side income target five hundred quid… call mum more…"

Messy. Rambling. Full of half-thoughts and backtracking. Exactly how real thinking sounds.

Wispr Flow turned all of it into clean text — punctuation, paragraphs, the lot. Then I spent two minutes grouping it: big goals, habits, admin. Done. Ten minutes, start to finish, and I had a monthly plan that actually reflected what I cared about.

Why talking beats typing for goals

Typing goals feels like writing a performance review for yourself. You self-censor. You write what sounds impressive instead of what's true.

Talking skips the filter. I said "call mum more" out loud — I'd never have typed that into a goals doc, but it's the one I'm most glad I captured. Voice gets you honesty; typing gets you a LinkedIn post.

And speaking forces priorities. When you only have your own attention for ten minutes, the important stuff surfaces first. The wedding venue made it into sentence two. The "redesign my Notion dashboard" idea never came up — which tells you everything about how much it mattered.

The result

One page. Five goals. Three habits. Zero blank-page dread. I stuck it on my wall where I'll actually see it.

If your monthly planning is a blank doc you've been avoiding, stop typing and start talking. Ten minutes, out loud, and October plans itself.
