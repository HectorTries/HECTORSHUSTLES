I Sent My Mum a Voice Note Every Day for a Week. She Loved It.

3 March 2026 • Productivity

My mum is not a texter. She calls. And honestly? I usually let it go to voicemail.

But I've been using Wispr Flow so much that I started sending voice notes to my mum instead of texts. Just quick updates about my day, thoughts, random stuff.

The results:

  • She actually responds now (with voice notes back!)
  • Feels more personal than a text
  • Takes 15 seconds instead of 5 minutes typing

She said it's like I'm actually talking to her, even though we're both busy. She's 73 and hates texting. Voice notes? She loves them.

It's weird - I can type a message in seconds but I never do. I can talk for 30 seconds and convey way more feeling.

If you want to stay closer to people who matter, try voice notes. It's not about the words - it's about the sound of your voice.

Start talking to the people you love.
