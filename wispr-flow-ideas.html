I Brainstormed 50 Video Ideas in 15 Minutes. Here's How.

2 March 2026 • Productivity

Ever sit down to create content and just... stare at a blank screen? Yeah, me too. The ideas are in your head somewhere, but trying to grab them feels like reaching into fog.

That's why I started brainstorming with Wispr Flow.

Instead of typing, I just talk. I ask myself questions out loud - "What would make a good video about?", "What problems did I solve this week?", "What annoyed me lately?" - and let the answers flow.

The results:

  • 50 ideas in 15 minutes (normally would take 2 hours)
  • They're messier, but that's good - the weird ones are often the best
  • I capture the energy behind the idea, not just the title

The trick is not to judge while you're talking. Just stream of consciousness. You can filter later.

I've found that talking out loud surfaces ideas I'd never type. The rhythm of speech triggers different thoughts than typing does. It's more associative, more chaotic, more creative.

Now when I need content ideas, I don't open a doc. I open Wispr Flow and just... talk.

Capture your ideas before they escape.
