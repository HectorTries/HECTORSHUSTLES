I Moved Flats Without Losing My Mind — I Dictated the Whole Checklist in 15 Minutes

27 October 2026 • Productivity

Moving house used to break me. Not the lifting. The list. Last time I moved, I had seventeen tabs open, three half-written notes, and a landlord text I never sent. I forgot to redirect my post, left cleaning supplies in the old oven, and paid for two broadband contracts for a month because I never cancelled the first one.

This move was different. I did the whole thing by talking.

I opened Wispr Flow on moving morning and just walked around the flat narrating: kitchen drawers, bathroom cabinet, bedroom wardrobe, hallway cupboard. Fifteen minutes of talking through every room while my hands stayed free to pack.

How it went

I went room by room and said what I saw. "Kitchen: mugs need boxes, mirror needs bubble wrap, defrost the freezer, photograph the meter. Bathroom: cabinet, shower rail, spare towels. Bedroom: wardrobe, charger behind the bed, documents folder." About fifteen minutes of narrating, no typing at all.

Then I kept talking and turned it into the admin batch. Council tax update, energy final reading email, landlord handover note, change-of-address list — bank, GP, driving licence, subscriptions. Said each one once, pasted the text straight into the forms.

Saying it aloud stopped things slipping. Typed lists go stale the second you leave the room. Spoken room by room, I caught the stuff I always forget — the spare keys, the router, the meter readings.

Why talking beats typing here

Moves live or die on completeness. Typing a checklist from memory the night before, you miss half the flat — then you are paying for two broadband contracts again.

Dictating while walking through the rooms meant the list matched reality. I saw it, I said it, it went on the list. No gaps, no guessing.

It also killed the dread. Moving admin felt like three evenings of misery in my head. Talking through it showed me I already knew every task — I just needed to say them once while looking at them.

The result

One clean list, zero "oh no I left something" moments. All admin done same-day instead of spread over two weeks. Meter photos taken, readings dictated into the handover email, keys labelled, flat cleaned.

Landlord replied same day: deposit back in full, no deductions. First move I have ever finished without a forgotten box or a double-paid bill.

Try it before your next move

Moving soon? Don't start with a blank page. Walk through your place, say what you see, and let the list build itself. Hands free, brain in the room, nothing left behind.
