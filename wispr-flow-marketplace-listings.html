I Listed 20 Items for Sale in 25 Minutes — By Talking to My Phone

9 October 2026 • Productivity

My 'sell pile' sat in the corner of my bedroom for four months. A pair of trainers, old headphones, jackets I'd stopped wearing, a bread maker I used exactly twice. Not because I didn't want the money — because writing listings is death by a thousand taps. "Nike trainers, size 9, worn twice, slight mark on left sole…" times twenty items? I'd finish one listing, lose the will to live, and go make tea instead.

Then I tried dictating my listings with Wispr Flow — and cleared the whole pile in a single evening.

How it works for me

I pick up the item, hit dictate, and describe it like I'm telling a mate about it: "Nike Revolution 6, size 9, black and white, worn maybe twice so the soles are basically new, tiny scuff on the left toe, only selling because they're half a size small for me. Smoke-free home, can post tomorrow." Thirty seconds. Wispr Flow handles the punctuation and cleanup, I paste it into Vinted, add the photos, and move to the next one.

Descriptions that used to take me five minutes of thumb-typing now take under a minute — and they're better, because when I talk I actually describe the thing instead of typing "good condition" and hoping for the best.

Why it works so well

Buyers search for specifics: brand, size, colour, condition details. A spoken description naturally includes all of that — measurements, flaws, why you're selling — because that's how you'd describe it to a person standing in front of you. Typed listings get lazy. Dictated ones stay detailed.

The detail pays twice. Listings with specifics sell faster and attract fewer "what size is this??" messages. And being upfront about flaws means no disputes, no returns, no awkward "this wasn't as described" conversations. My buyer ratings have never been higher.

The numbers

Before dictation: 3 items listed in four months, £18 earned, and a mountain of guilt every time I looked at the pile. After one dictated evening: 20 listings live, built in about 25 minutes of actual describing (plus photo time). Within a week, 11 had sold for £142 total. Time per listing dropped from roughly 8 minutes to about 90 seconds. The pile is gone. My bedroom floor is visible again.

Try it this week

Look at the nearest thing you own and don't use. Pick it up, dictate an honest 30-second description, and list it tonight. Just one. Once you feel how fast it is, the rest of the pile doesn't stand a chance.

If you haven't tried voice dictation yet, give Wispr Flow a go with my link — it's free to start, and it turned my four-month sell pile into £142 in a week. Your wardrobe is sitting on money.
