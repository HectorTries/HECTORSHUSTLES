I Stopped Typing My Emails and Used My Voice Instead. Here's How it Changed Everything.

2 March 2026 • Productivity

Let's be honest, nobody enjoys writing emails. For years, it felt like a mandatory daily chore, a mental tax I had to pay before I could get to the real work. I realised I was losing about 30 minutes every single morning just to typing out responses that could have taken five. That's 2.5 hours a week, just on email, not counting the mental energy drain. The flow was gone before I even started.

Then, I discovered Wispr Flow.

This isn't just another voice-to-text tool that spits out a mess of transcription errors. This is different. You hit one button, speak naturally, and your words appear on the screen – accurately, almost instantly. It captures your actual voice, your natural cadence, the energy behind what you're trying to say.

I decided to put it to the test. Instead of my usual laborious typing, I committed to dictating every single email for a full day.

My first email of the day was to my accountant, asking about a complex tax query. Normally, I'd overthink every sentence, trying to sound professional and precise. With Wispr Flow, I just spoke it out as I would explain it to a friend. The result? A natural, conversational email that got my point across clearly and concisely, in literally half the time it would usually take. No more stiff, robotic-sounding messages.

Here's what happened after sending 10 emails using just my voice:

  • Time Saved: My morning email ritual, which typically devoured 30 minutes, was cut down to a lean 10. That's a huge win, freeing up precious time for deeper work.
  • Authenticity Restored: The biggest revelation wasn't just speed; it was the tone. Typing often forces a formality that can make emails sound cold or generic. Dictating with Wispr Flow allowed my natural speaking voice to come through. My emails sounded like me, like a real human wrote them, which led to more genuine interactions.
  • Energy Levels: Instead of feeling mentally drained after the email slog, I felt energised. The cognitive load of typing, spell-checking, and rephrasing was gone. I could focus purely on the message, not the mechanics of delivery.
  • Clarity & Conciseness: When you speak, you tend to be more direct. I found my emails became sharper, cutting through the fluff I often added when typing.

The real game-changer isn't just about saving time; it's about making your communication more human, more efficient, and frankly, more enjoyable. It's wild to think such a simple shift could make such a profound difference.

If you're like me and emails are a significant part of your day, or if you just want your writing to sound more like you, you owe it to yourself to try this. The setup takes less than a minute. Then, just talk.
