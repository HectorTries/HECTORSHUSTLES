I Turned My Car Into a Thinking Studio.

3 March 2026 • Productivity

I spend 45 minutes driving every day. Used to be dead time—radio, podcasts, just noise. Now? It's my most productive thinking hour.

Here's the trick: Wispr Flow in the car. Bluetooth connected, phone mounted, voice ready.

The results:

  • 7 business ideas captured during commutes this week
  • 0 distractions - no phone in hand, just voice
  • Better thinking - something about movement helps

I solved 2 problems I've been stuck on for weeks. Just by talking through them out loud while driving.

The car is the last truly distraction-free space. You're stuck there anyway. Might as well think out loud.

Next time you're in traffic, try it. Your commute might be your best thinking time.
