I Write My Newsletter in 20 Minutes Now — By Talking It Out First

10 October 2026 • Productivity

I started a newsletter in January with big plans: every Sunday, useful stuff, no fluff. By March I'd published twice. Not because I had nothing to say — because every issue meant staring at a blank draft for two hours, typing a paragraph, deleting it, and telling myself I'd "finish it tomorrow."

Then I started dictating my first drafts with Wispr Flow — and I haven't missed a Sunday since.

How it works for me

I go for a Saturday walk, hit dictate, and talk through the week's issue like I'm explaining it to a friend: the one idea, the story behind it, the thing I learned. Ten minutes of rambling. Wispr Flow handles the punctuation and cleanup, and when I get home I've got 600 messy words waiting — already structured, already in my voice.

Then I edit. Cutting a messy spoken draft into a tight 400-word issue takes me about ten minutes. Writing that same issue from scratch used to take two hours, most of it spent rewording the first sentence.

Why it works so well

Newsletters die from perfectionism, not lack of ideas. A blank page invites you to sound clever. Talking invites you to sound like yourself — and readers subscribe for you, not for polished essays. My spoken drafts have more stories, more opinions, and shorter sentences than anything I typed.

Consistency compounds too. Weekly issues beat brilliant monthly ones. Since I switched to dictating, my open rate went from 38% to 52%, and three readers have replied saying the newsletter "feels more personal lately." It is. It's literally me talking.

The numbers

Before dictation: 2 issues in 8 weeks, each taking 2+ hours, open rate 38%. After dictation: 12 issues in 12 weeks, each taking about 20 minutes total (10 talking, 10 editing), open rate 52%, subscriber count up from 140 to 310. Same ideas. Same Sundays. One-tenth of the effort.

Try it this week

Open a blank note, hit dictate, and talk through one thing you learned this week for five minutes. Don't edit while you talk. Then clean it up and send it to one friend. That's your first issue.

If you haven't tried voice dictation yet, give Wispr Flow a go with my link — it's free to start, and it turned my dead newsletter into a 12-week streak I'm proud of. Your readers are waiting.
