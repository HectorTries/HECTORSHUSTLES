I Cleared 47 Emails in 18 Minutes — By Talking to My Inbox

10 September 2026 • Productivity

I used to dread opening Gmail. 50+ unread, half of them needing actual replies, the rest newsletters I was too guilty to delete. I'd block out an hour, type two replies, get distracted, and quit.

Last Tuesday I tried something different: inbox-zero triage with Wispr Flow.

Here's the setup. I open an email, hit my Wispr hotkey, and just talk: "Reply to Sarah, yes Thursday works for the call, send her the Cullify link, keep it short and friendly." Wispr types a clean draft — punctuation, names, links all correct — and I hit send. Next email.

For the junk? Even faster: "Archive, no reply needed." Done.

47 emails. 18 minutes. 12 replies sent, 28 archived, 7 flagged for later. Inbox zero before my coffee went cold.

Why does voice work so much better here? Because email replies are mostly thinking, not writing. I know what I want to say — "yes, Thursday, here's the link" — but typing it out formally takes 3 minutes per email. Dictating it takes 20 seconds. Wispr Flow is built for exactly this: it handles natural speech, fixes my "ums," formats as I speak, and works right inside Gmail, Superhuman, wherever.

My system now is stupid simple:

  • Two-minute rule, voiced. If I can answer in one breath, I dictate and send immediately.
  • Whisper for the tricky ones. Wispr has a whisper mode — perfect for dictating a polite "no" to a sales rep while my girlfriend's on a call in the next room.
  • Commands for triage. "Archive all newsletters from this sender" — said once, typed perfectly.

The result isn't just speed. My replies are warmer, more human. When I type, I go full robot: "Acknowledged. Will review." When I talk, I sound like me: "Love this, thanks for sending it over!"

I tracked it for a week: I was spending ~45 minutes a day on email. Now it's ~15. That's 3.5 hours a week back for Fortune & Folly scripts, for Cullify, for actual life.

If your inbox stresses you out, try talking to it instead of typing at it.

Your future inbox-zero self will thank you.
