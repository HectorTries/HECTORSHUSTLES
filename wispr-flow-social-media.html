I Wrote 30 Days of Social Media Posts in One Morning. Here's How.

2 March 2026 • Productivity

Social media is a grind. You're supposed to be everywhere at once - Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads. Each platform needs a different tone, different length, different vibe. By the time you've adapted your message five times, you've lost the energy to actually post.

That's if you're typing.

I've been using Wispr Flow to dictate my social media, and it's completely changed how I show up online. Instead of staring at a blank box trying to craft the perfect tweet, I just talk.

Want to know the difference?

Before (typing):

  • 30 minutes to write 5 posts
  • Feels like work
  • Posts sound formal and stiff
  • Eventually just give up and don't post

After (dictating):

  • 20 minutes to record 15 posts
  • Feels like having a conversation
  • Posts sound like me
  • Actually enjoy showing up

The magic isn't just the speed. It's that when you talk, your personality comes through. That inside joke lands better when you're saying it naturally. That hot take sounds more passionate when you're actually ranting.

I dictated a thread on Bitcoin finance the other day. 12 tweets. Took me 8 minutes. Engagement was higher than anything I've typed in months.

If you're building an audience online, your voice is your unfair advantage. Don't kill it by typing.

Try Wispr Flow and let your voice be heard.
